I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from [Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET)](https://www.cuet.ac.bd), where I graduated with a CGPA of **3.75/4.00** and received the **Dean's Award** for academic excellence.

My research interests span **Natural Language Processing**, **Large Language Models**, **Low-Resource Language Understanding**, **Multimodal Learning**, and **Responsible AI**. During my undergraduate years, I worked in the NLP Research Lab at CUET under [Dr. Mohammed Moshiul Hoque](https://scholar.google.com/), contributing to published work at **NAACL 2025** and **COLING 2025** on fake news detection, hate speech identification, and misogyny meme classification. My thesis focused on stress identification from Bengali social media texts using lexicon-augmented Transformer models.

On the engineering side, I currently work as a **Software Engineer at AsthaIT Inc.**, where I build and maintain [Apex4u.com](https://apex4u.com) — a high-traffic e-commerce platform — using **ASP.NET Core**, **Next.js**, and **AWS**. Previously, I designed a **RAG-based** intelligent document Q&A system (Taxinument) at Inument Solutions using **LangChain**, **GPT-4**, and **Qdrant**.

I am also an active competitive programmer with **1200+ problems solved** and a Codeforces rating of **1527 (Specialist)**.
